## 🧑‍💻 About me

My background combines **data analytics and engineering, Business Intelligence, and reporting automation**. I've worked with processes across startups, banking, Oil & Gas, government, social assistance, and agronomy.

Today, I bring that experience to **Odoo 19** development, service integration, and business process automation. I enjoy following the full journey of data: extracting it, giving it structure, analyzing it, and turning it into useful tools for everyday operations.

🎓 I'm currently **training at TripleTen to become a Data Scientist**, expanding my knowledge of statistics and machine learning to answer business questions.
